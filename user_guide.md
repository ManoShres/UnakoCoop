# User Guide: Share & Dividend Distribution Suite

## Overview
This feature provides an intuitive, reliable, and compliant way to execute **Dividend Distributions** (नगद लाभांश वितरण) and **Bonus Share Allotments** (बोनस सेयर बाँडफाँड) across Unako SACCOS.

---

## 1. For Cooperative Administrators & Tellers

### A. Bulk AGM Dividend Distribution (एकमुष्ठ साधारण सभा लाभांश वितरण)
1. Navigate to **Admin Dashboard → Shares & Mudhati Suite (सेयर पुँजी तथा मुद्दती निक्षेप)**.
2. In the top action bar, click the blue button **"वार्षिक लाभांश वितरण (+ Distribute AGM Dividends)"**.
3. In the modal:
   - Review or modify the **Dividend Rate %** (e.g. `14.5%` or AGM approved rate).
   - Ensure the **5% Statutory Dividend Tax Deduction (TDS)** toggle is enabled per Nepal Tax regulations.
   - Choose the payout target: **Direct Credit to Regular Savings (बचत खातामा जम्मा)** or **Accrue in Member Ledger (बक्यौता खातामा राख्ने)**.
   - Click **"Preview Distribution List (बाँडफाँड विवरण हेर्नुहोस्)"** to review individual shareholder payouts, tax withheld, and net amounts.
   - Click **"Confirm & Distribute Now (लाभांश वितरण प्रमाणित गर्नुहोस्)"**.
4. The system will automatically:
   - Credit each member's savings account or accrued balance.
   - Generate official `DIVIDEND` transactions with reference numbers (`DIV-2081-XXXX`).
   - Post an official announcement on the member dashboard noticeboard.

### B. Bonus Share Distribution (बोनस सेयर कित्ता बाँडफाँड)
1. In the Shares Management suite, click **"बोनस सेयर बाँडफाँड (+ Distribute Bonus Shares)"**.
2. Enter the approved Bonus Share percentage (e.g. `5%` or `10%`).
3. Preview the total additional kitta being created and the new total share capital.
4. Click **"Distribute Bonus Shares (कित्ता बाँडफाँड गर्नुहोस्)"**.
5. All shareholder accounts will have their share kitta and share capital updated immediately at NPR 100/kitta.

### C. Single-Member Dividend Payout (व्यक्तिगत सदस्य लाभांश भुक्तानी)
1. In the **Member Share Holdings Register** table, locate the member.
2. Under the **Action (कार्य)** column, click the **"लाभांश भुक्तानी (Pay Dividend)"** button.
3. Review the member's accrued or annual dividend, confirm destination (Savings Account or Cash Counter Voucher), and click **"Disburse (भुक्तानी गर्नुहोस्)"**.

---

## 2. For Cooperative Members (Member Portal)

### A. Claim Accrued Dividend (लाभांश बचतमा स्थानान्तरण)
1. Log in to the **Member Portal** and go to **Shares & Dividends (सेयर पुँजी तथा लाभांश)**.
2. When you have an unclaimed or accrued dividend, a banner will appear showing your available balance (e.g. `NPR 27,800.00`).
3. Click **"लाभांश बचतमा जम्मा गर्नुहोस् (Transfer to Regular Savings)"**.
4. Confirm your target savings account. The funds are instantly deposited into your passbook and available for withdrawal or transfers.

### B. Reinvest Dividend in Additional Shares (लाभांशबाट सेयर खरिद)
1. Click **"सेयरमा पुँजीकरण गर्नुहोस् (Reinvest in Shares)"**.
2. Convert your dividend directly into additional cooperative equity units at par value (NPR 100 per kitta) without paying any transaction fees.
